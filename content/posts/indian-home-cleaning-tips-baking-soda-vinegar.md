Keep your home spotless with simple baking soda, vinegar and lemon hacks that clean shower heads, tile gaps, microwaves, fridges, kitchen napkins and even smelly shoes — using things already in your kitchen.

These are tried-and-tested cleaning tips for Indian homes, focused on the small things that daily wiping never reaches: clogged shower holes, water-stained taps, grimy tile grooves, lingering microwave smells. The same two ingredients — baking soda and white vinegar — solve most of them.

## How do I unclog a shower head with weak water force?

If the water pressure from your shower has dropped, the most likely reason is mineral buildup blocking the tiny holes in the shower head. Soap and a brush won't clear it.

1. Mix baking soda and vinegar into a paste-like solution.
2. Pour the mixture into a plastic bag.
3. Tie the bag around the shower head so the holes are fully submerged.
4. Leave it for a few hours.
5. Remove the bag and scrub the head with any old brush.

The holes reopen and water force returns to normal.

## How should I clean toothbrushes and the brush holder?

Bathroom items like toothbrushes and their holders need weekly attention — they sit in moisture all day and collect residue.

- **Brushes:** once a week, place each brush separately in its own glass of hot water. This sanitises the bristles.
- **Holder:** clean with the baking soda and vinegar mixture, or with regular dishwashing soap. Baking soda cleans more thoroughly and needs minimal scrubbing.

## What's the best way to clean a wash basin and remove tap water stains?

We wipe the wash basin daily, but stubborn water stains on taps don't lift with normal soap. When you're already cleaning the shower with the baking soda and vinegar mixture, use the same mixture on the wash basin and tap. Hard-water marks, soap scum, and dullness all clear up, and the fittings shine again.

## How do I clean the dirty gaps between bathroom and kitchen tiles?

The grooves between tiles trap dirt that daily mopping never reaches.

1. Mix baking soda with vinegar.
2. Apply along the tile gaps.
3. Scrub with any old brush — an unused toothbrush works perfectly.

The fizzing reaction loosens the buildup, and a few minutes of brushing brings the white grout back.

## How do I remove food smell from the microwave?

Even a freshly cleaned microwave can hold the smell of a particular food. The fix is free: lemon peels you'd otherwise throw away.

- Drop used lemon peels into a bowl of water.
- Microwave for one minute — ideally at night before bed.
- Leave it closed overnight.

By morning, the microwave smells fresh and faintly of lemon.

### Can I use the same trick for smelly glass tumblers?

Yes. Glass tumblers sometimes hold a stale smell. Either rub the inside with lemon peels and wash, or fill them with hot water and a peel and let them sit for a while. The smell disappears completely.

## How often should I wash kitchen napkins and how do I clean them properly?

Kitchen napkins do the heaviest lifting in any Indian kitchen — wiping oil, masala splashes, wet hands, and counter spills all day. A normal rinse won't fully clean them.

Once a week, soak them in hot water with a little dishwashing liquid. The hot water + detergent combination breaks down the trapped oil and removes any lingering smell. They come out almost like new.

## Why should I clean the rubber seal on the fridge door?

The rubber gasket on the fridge door is one of the most ignored parts of the kitchen. Grime accumulates in its folds and affects how well the door seals. Cleaning the inside of the fridge daily isn't realistic — but whenever you do clean the fridge, take a scrubber, apply a little soap, scrub the rubber, and wipe it down with a damp cloth. It cleans up easily and stays hygienic.

## How do I remove bad smell from shoes in summer without spending money?

In hot weather, shoes pick up odour that even sun-drying doesn't always fix. Two zero-cost solutions you already have at home:

1. **Used tea bags** — drop a couple of dried, used tea bags inside each shoe and leave them overnight.
2. **Silica sachets** — the small sachets that come packed inside a new thermos, water bottle, or shoe box. Save them and reuse them inside shoes.

Both absorb moisture and smell, completely solving the shoe-odour problem without any product purchase.

## What's the one cleaning mixture worth keeping ready in an Indian home?

A simple **baking soda + vinegar** mix. From this single video alone, it cleans:

- Shower heads
- Toothbrush holders
- Wash basins
- Taps with hard-water stains
- Kitchen and bathroom tile grout

Keep both ingredients stocked and you'll rarely need a specialised bathroom cleaner.

You can make a small home far easier to live in with a handful of low-cost, problem-specific fixes — motion-sensor lights, mesh laundry bags, foldable clip hangers, stronger adhesive hooks, hinge spray, and a transparent door-gap strip — rather than one big expensive overhaul.

Home-making today means juggling children, cleaning, and keeping everything organized at once. The ideas below each target one everyday annoyance in an Indian home, and almost all are cheaply available on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, or at a Miniso store.

## How do I find my way around the house at night without fumbling for a switch?

Motion-sensor induction lamps solve the middle-of-the-night problem — waking up thirsty or needing the washroom and having to hunt for your phone or torch in the dark before you can even leave the room.

These lamps turn on automatically as soon as they detect your movement and switch off on their own after a few seconds. They are useful beyond the bedroom: in the kitchen and bathroom, night-time switches are hard to see and easy to miss when you are half asleep.

Installing them is simple:

1. Peel the pre-attached adhesive sticker on the back of the lamp.
2. Fix it to the wall, or use the attached magnet to mount it under the bed or on any metal surface (both sides have magnets).
3. Set it to automatic mode so it responds to movement — there is also a manual on/off button if you prefer.

## How do I stop socks from getting lost in the wash?

Small items like socks wander during a wash, get tangled in larger clothes, and lose their pairs. The fix is a mesh (netted) laundry bag.

Put your socks or other small items inside, zip it shut, and wash the whole bag exactly as you wash your regular clothes in the machine. Nothing mixes, tangles, or wanders off. These come in sets of two, three, or four — inexpensive and easily found online, or at a Miniso store if one has opened in your city.

## What is the easiest way to dry socks and small clothes?

Drying small items is its own problem — they slip off ropes and you cannot clip many at once. A foldable clip hanger fixes this. Each clip holds two or three small items, and you can add extra clips as needed; it usually comes with a couple of spare hooks too.

The best part is that it folds away when not in use, so it takes no space and frees up the drying area for other clothes. Keep it outside, and during the rainy season bring it in quickly so your clothes do not get wet.

## Why do my adhesive hooks keep falling off?

Adhesive hooks fall for two reasons: they are overloaded, or they are used too soon after sticking. Switching to stronger, larger hooks rated for up to 2 kg makes a real difference — some packs include four different shapes (a large round hook, an oval hook, and two medium round ones) for different jobs.

Keep these two habits in mind:

1. Always check the weight capacity printed on the hook and hang only items within that limit — a wiper or mop at around 1 kg sits comfortably under a 2 kg rating.
2. After sticking a hook, wait at least 40 minutes to an hour before hanging anything on it, so the adhesive sets fully.

If a tool's hanging hole is too small for the hook, a little home *jugaad* — tying a short rope through it — works perfectly. Hanging mops, wipers, and dusting tools this way keeps the bathroom looking tidy instead of messy.

There are also small decorative clips (three for around ₹100) that can hang a towel or hand towel in the kitchen or bathroom. They do not come with wall adhesive, so use strong transparent double-sided tape to mount them. A hand towel is light, so they should hold for a long time.

## How do I stop a door or window from squeaking?

Doors squeak more over time as rust builds up in the joints. A liquid lubricant spray stops the noise almost instantly — spray it on the joints inside and outside, and the sound is gone at once.

Avoid plain cooking oil for this. Oil often attracts ants in large numbers, while the spray does not. It works on doors, windows, and even cabinet doors, and is easy to find on Amazon or at any electrical or hardware shop.

## How can I seal the gap under or above my door?

Gaps at the top or bottom of a door are entry points for lizards, insects, and even mice. A self-adhesive transparent rubber gap strip closes them neatly:

1. Peel off the backing sticker.
2. Measure the length of your door's gap.
3. Cut the strip to that length.
4. Press it onto the door, at the top or bottom as needed.

Because it is transparent, you cannot tell anything has been applied once it is on — even from outside, the gap is fully sealed. Rubber does not deteriorate with weather, so it lasts, and you can install it yourself without any help.

## How do I keep my handbag organized?

When a bag is stuffed with loose items, finding anything small becomes impossible. A bag organizer insert, sized to match your bag, keeps everything in its place so whatever you need is right where it should be. Inserts come in sizes for both small and large bags.

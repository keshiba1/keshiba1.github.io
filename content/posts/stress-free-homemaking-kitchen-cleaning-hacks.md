Most everyday household frustrations — greasy stove knobs, blocked sinks, pen stains, wall scribbles — can be solved in minutes using tools you already own at home.

This video walks through a healthy morning ladoo and a set of low-cost cleaning fixes for the ten small problems Indian homemakers run into daily. Every hack below uses cheap, already-at-home items like a toothbrush, cotton buds, Dettol, toothpaste and hot water.

## How do I make a healthy ladoo for kids every morning?

Four ingredients matter most: almonds, *makhana* (fox nuts), fennel seeds (*saunf*) and sesame seeds (*til*) — white or black, whichever you have. Everything else is optional.

1. Take a little *ghee* and lightly roast your dry fruits — cashews, almonds, *makhana*, coarse fennel and sesame. Roast just until crisp; don't let them brown or burn.
2. Add optional extras if you have them: raisins, walnuts, pistachios, musk melon seeds and pumpkin seeds. Roast lightly and mix in.
3. In a separate pan with a generous amount of *ghee*, roast flour until its colour changes and a nice aroma rises. Wheat flour (*atta*), *ragi* flour or *sooji* (semolina) all work.
4. Let the roasted dry fruits cool completely, then grind them into a fine powder along with *mishri* (rock sugar) — a healthier choice than plain sugar — and a little oats.
5. Mix the ground powder into the warm *ghee*-roasted flour while it's still slightly warm. Wear gloves if the heat bothers your hands.
6. Add two teaspoons of cardamom powder, and optionally nutmeg or cinnamon powder, then shape into ladoos.

Give kids one ladoo every morning before school for stronger bones and added health benefits. The exact measurements are shared in the video's comments and description.

## How do I clean dirty stove knobs the easy way?

Oil and food bits build up inside gas stove knobs and feel impossible to reach. Here's the quick method:

1. Gently pull the knobs off — they come away easily.
2. Spray them with any kitchen cleaner, a homemade spray, or plain soapy water.
3. Scrub with a small brush. Keep three or four different brushes near your sink for these small jobs.
4. For deep grime, use a thin brush or a soft-bristled toothbrush that reaches into corners without scratching the surface.
5. If a brush won't fit, use cotton buds to pull out grime and trapped oil from deep inside.
6. Wipe down with a damp cloth.

A job that looks difficult finishes in seconds. Keep a fresh, unused toothbrush aside just for kitchen cleaning — never one you've already brushed with. Used toothbrushes are fine for floors and other surfaces.

One important step: after spraying, the knobs stay damp for a while. Let them dry out fully before refitting. A knob fitted back wet may never dry inside, and that trapped moisture lets grime keep building up. Once dry, fit them back and turn on the gas once to confirm everything works.

All these brushes are easily available on Amazon, Flipkart or any local shop.

## Why does my kitchen sink keep getting blocked?

Sink drains block up because oily, greasy liquids stick to the pipe walls and slowly narrow them, which also causes a smell. The rule is simple: don't drain oily or greasy things.

If you accidentally pour oil down the drain, immediately follow it with good boiling hot water so the grease is flushed off the pipe walls before it hardens. Plain water alone won't shift stuck-on oil.

## How do I wash greasy ghee pans without ruining my scrubber?

Indian kitchens cook plenty of *ghee*- and oil-based dishes, so vessels like a *kadhai* used to roast flour come out heavily greased. Don't sit down to wash them straight away.

Pour good hot water into the pan and let it sit until the *ghee* fully loosens, then scrub. Washing directly leaves the vessel poorly cleaned and coats your scrubber in grease — which then needs its own hot-water soak. Soaking first saves that double effort.

## How do I remove pen and ink stains from clothes?

Refill-pen and ink stains on kids' or a husband's shirts often survive even a machine wash. This method needs no scrubbing soap at first:

1. Put a little Dettol directly on the pen, sketch-pen or pencil stain.
2. Leave it for about 2 minutes.
3. Scrub the spot without any soap or water — the marks lift off as you scrub.
4. Once the stains are gone, wash normally with water and soap.

Even a white shirt comes out completely clean, with no ink left in any corner and no damage to the fabric.

## How do I clean crayon and pen marks off walls?

Kids' wall drawings in pen, pencil, crayon or sketch pen come off with toothpaste:

1. Put regular toothpaste — any brand you already own — on an old toothbrush.
2. Without adding water, gently scrub the marks.
3. Keep the pressure light. Scrubbing too hard can lift the wall paint too.
4. Once the marks are gone, wipe the area with a damp cloth.

The wall looks spotless again, with no sign a drawing was ever there.

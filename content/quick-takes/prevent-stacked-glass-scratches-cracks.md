To stop stacked glasses from getting scratched or cracked, slip a plastic or paper cup between each one as you stack them. It's easy to look at the things piling up in your kitchen and decide to give them away or throw them out — but the moment you toss glass items to save storage space, the ones you keep tend to pick up scratches and even cracks from rubbing together. A simple cup buffer between each glass cushions them so they stay flawless while still stacking compactly.

The same smart-homemaker thinking fixes a messy towel hook. A towel hung straight on a hook looks untidy and keeps slipping down. Instead, slide a pretty bangle or bracelet onto the hook and hang the towel through it — it stays neat, looks nice, and if it ever slips you can just pin it in place.

The simplest way to make your home look organized is to put back whatever you pick up, right where it belongs — no amount of cleaning helps if used things stay scattered. From that one habit flow the rest: store onions with a rolled-up paper tucked among them so they don't spoil; add dry *poha* to the bottom of your salt container to fight moisture, and always stir salt with a wooden spoon since metal reacts with it. Refill jars through a paper cone to keep the counter spill-free. Label look-alike oils with plain white tape so no one confuses the olive oil again. Hang used towels on a stand, behind doors, or in the bathroom so they dry quickly and smell-free, and use cheap over-door hangers for clothes you'll wear again. Corral small packets and daily medicines in little baskets instead of leaving them on the counter. For cockroaches, a gel from any general store works best.
